The first three source files were fetched from pinned raw URLs and SHA256 recorded. The CC legal-code website refused a direct plain-text fetch; the bundled license text uses the Creative Commons official source repository. External RCSF evidence is a pinned reference, not a fresh claim that its runtime or all semantics have passed this library's tests.

Original fixtures and mathematical oracles are project authorship. Their expectations use analytical rotations, Hermite basis coefficients and elementary affine sums. Numeric fixture tolerances are project acceptance choices. Full visual/artist contact acceptance, additional format support and provider calibration remain separate evidence gaps.
